Privacy policy

Lumina: privacy policy
Last updated: 25 November 2026

Lumina is a tarot app by ECHOx. This policy says what Lumina does with what you give it, where it goes, and how long it stays.

Who is responsible

The sole trader named above makes Lumina under the name ECHOx and is responsible for your personal data (the controller). Apple and Google are responsible for their stores and payments. The services named in this policy process data for Lumina as it describes.
Contact for privacy questions: inbox@echoxstudios.art

The short version

  • The app uses a pseudonymous reading account to manage subscriptions, credits and session passes. It has no advertising, analytics or tracking. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Your personal reading archive and diary are stored on your device. Online readings send information to the services described below. The wallet temporarily keeps some reading content for retries. Device backups and copies you export may survive app deletion.
  • To read your cards, what you send (a photograph of your spread, your question, what you say in the conversation) passes through Lumina's server to the AI service that writes the reading. The wallet keeps the opening reply for retry protection as described below.
  • If you book a live session, your name, email address, note and the time are kept so the session can happen.
  • Live calls are not recorded.

What is sent, and why

Anthropic's published API policy provides for deletion of API inputs and outputs within 30 days. If its automated safety systems flag a request, Anthropic may keep the inputs and outputs for up to two years and the safety classification for up to seven years. Lumina does not send feedback ratings to Anthropic. The local archive and the provider's processing have different retention periods. Anthropic’s API retention policy

A reading. When you photograph your spread, the photograph goes to Lumina's server, which asks Anthropic's AI service (Claude) which cards are in it. When you ask for a reading, your question, the cards and their positions, and what you say in the conversation go the same way, so that the reading can be written. The reading server returns the answer. The wallet caches the opening reply for interrupted requests. While the wallet server is running, a cleanup check runs every five minutes. It removes cached opening text older than one day and retains a transaction marker. It records a fingerprint of the draw and the credit transaction. It does not keep a transcript of subsequent ordinary messages. Anthropic processes them on Lumina's behalf and, under its terms for business customers, does not use them to train its models. So that the next reply in the same conversation costs less, Anthropic may hold the unchanged opening part of a request in its prompt cache for up to an hour. The reading server keeps a usage record for each paid step: the reading's random identifier, the time, the kind of step and the amount of model text used, for cost accounting. The record holds no question, card, photograph or message. After the account is deleted, it no longer connects to anyone.

Lumina Academy. The Academy is not offered yet. When it opens, this applies. Lesson and flashcard progress, practice drafts and feedback are kept on your device. Self-study, picture recall and dictionary lookups run locally. When purchasing or restoring the course, the app asks your device’s store about the purchase. It keeps a local ownership flag for offline access. Asking the fictional sitter to reply sends the exercise situation and your opening to the AI service. Asking for coaching sends the practice dialogue and a short description of the table you arranged. A human does not grade these readings. When you request a certificate, the service checks your completed exercise identifiers. It stores the name you want printed, your delivery email and a private order reference before checkout. It verifies the purchase with your store and keeps the purchase reference and issued PDF for delivery and recovery. Resend receives your delivery address and the named PDF to send the attachment. Your practice dialogue stays out of the order.

The development steps. The cards you choose, what you say about them and the card derived locally in steps that use your date of birth go to Lumina's server and on to Anthropic in the same way, so that Lumina can talk with you about the cards and write your presentation. When you take a later step, what you chose and said in your earlier steps (your own words, not Lumina's) is sent with it, so that Lumina can see the steps together. The app saves the development archive on your device. The providers process the submitted information as described above.

Speaking instead of typing. Microphone buttons use the device's native speech-recognition service, under the platform's permissions and privacy terms. The current app does not upload dictation audio to Lumina's server or its transcription API. The resulting text is sent only as part of the action you submit, like typed text. Native recognition may use the platform provider's servers; offline operation is not guaranteed.

A live session. Live sessions are not offered yet. When they open, this applies. When you book, the server keeps your name, email address, any note you add, the session and its time, and a booking code. The reader sees them in their list of sessions. Your name, email address and note are removed 90 days after the session; the booking code, time and session stay for our accounts. The video call runs through LiveKit, whose region this policy will name before the first session can be booked; it carries the picture and sound between you and the reader while the call lasts and nothing of it is recorded or kept.

Payment. All in-app purchases, including live sessions and certificates, use App Store or Google Play billing. The store processes the payment. Lumina receives purchase references to verify access and does not receive your card details.

What stays on your phone

Your ordinary reading records contain the full conversation, the final card positions, turns, arrows and labels. A photographed reading also keeps its spread images on this device. Opening the record is read-only. Your readings, your steps and where you left off, your date of birth if you gave it, and your booked sessions' codes. They are not backed up to Lumina. They are removed when you delete them in the app or delete the app.

Reporting a response. If you choose to report a reply, the team receives the text you type or paste and your pseudonymous account reference. Leave out identifying details about yourself or other people. The report goes to the operator for review. It is removed by the service’s scheduled cleanup after 30 days or when you delete the account. The rest of your conversation and photos are not attached.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what Lumina holds about you, have it corrected or deleted, receive a copy, or object to its use, by writing to inbox@echoxstudios.art. For a booking, give its code. For a certificate order, use the delivery email and include the certificate reference if you have it. You may also complain to a data protection authority, including the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or the competent authority where you live.

A reading, its credits, the membership, a booked session and a certificate are processed to perform the contract you enter (GDPR article 6(1)(b)). A reading can reveal your health, beliefs or relationships. Lumina sends that content to Anthropic only with the explicit consent you give before each AI reading (article 9(2)(a)), and you can withdraw it by not starting or by stopping a reading. Purchase and tax records are kept to meet legal obligations (article 6(1)(c)). Security, fraud prevention, abuse reports and the age check for connections from the USA rely on legitimate interests (article 6(1)(f)): keeping the service and its users safe, and meeting New York's law, which is not the law of the EU or a member state and so is not a legal obligation in the sense of article 6(1)(c). The optional inactivity email relies on your consent (article 6(1)(a)); you withdraw it by removing the address. Device camera and microphone permissions control hardware access.

Children

Lumina is not meant for anyone under 18.

The age check in the United States. New York's law on AI companions requires an age check beyond your own word before a reading reaches someone in New York. A connection does not show reliably which state you are in, so Lumina asks for the check whenever your connection's approximate location or your store account is in the USA, or the location cannot be told. The wallet keeps only that an adult was confirmed, when and by which method, and deletes it with your account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with its date, and the app says so where it matters.

Membership and Plus. The reading account stores a random account reference, verified benefits, purchase references, credits and reading reservations. The daily reading sends the chosen card, orientation and optional context to the reader. Your phone receives the note with the first result so it can save them together. For new readings the wallet keeps the card details and generated reply for retries. It omits the separate note field. A reply may reflect what you wrote. This cache is removed by the same cleanup check after three days. The account keeps a time zone to establish its reading day. Diary text and links to readings stay on the device. Removing a linked reading preserves the diary entry.

My Lumina and export. The personal archive is device-local. An exported ordinary reading contains the final board and the available reading summary or closing text. It opens as an offline HTML file in the folder you choose. A new phone does not download these records from Lumina. People, labels and circumstances can still be identifiable in a summary or board. Store exports somewhere appropriate for private material.

Online account deletion and optional inactivity email. You can delete your online reading account in My Lumina. This removes the wallet profile and its operational rows. Manage subscriptions separately through your store. The personal archive remains under your control on the device; remove individual records there. The store and independently fulfilled bookings or certificate orders have separate retention and rights processes. Lumina's server is backed up daily and each backup is deleted after seven days, so data you delete leaves the backups within a week. If you enable and verify an inactivity address, the service uses it for account notices. An eligible unused account can be removed after six calendar months of inactivity and a seven-day return period following confirmed email delivery. Active subscriptions, remaining credits, an owned course and unused passes are excluded. Opening My Lumina on the original account cancels the countdown. Without a verified address there is no emailed-deletion countdown. This email is optional and can be removed.

Checking paid readings. For retail memberships the wallet keeps your store transaction reference or Play purchase token in the private account database. It uses the store to check the membership when the account is refreshed and before providing an online reading. Expired membership verification references are removed by the running cleanup after 31 days. Removing the account deletes the references from the live database. Store purchase records and backup copies follow their own retention terms. When you request a paid reading, the gateway estimates your country and, where necessary, subdivision from your connection address using a database on the host. The wallet does not add your address or location to the reading archive. Connection logs on the server, which contain IP addresses, are kept for at most 14 days for security.

An optional wallet recovery code lets you move access to your purchases to a new device. Keep it private. The server stores its hash. Recovery replaces the previous device credential. Reading files stay on the original device.
