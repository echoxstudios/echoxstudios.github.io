Safety in Lumina

How Lumina keeps a reading safe
Last updated: 25 November 2026

Lumina is an AI

Lumina, the app’s tarot reader, is an AI. The app says so before every AI reading, under every reading and in About. Asked whether it is a person, Lumina says that it is an AI tarot reader, and it never claims to be human. A reading is personal reflection through tarot, for personal development and as a hobby or entertainment. It is not fortune telling, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, legal advice or financial advice. Lumina never foresees or promises anything, and the decisions stay yours.

For adults only

Lumina is for adults aged 18 and over and is not suitable for minors. Where the law requires it, the app asks Apple or Google for your age range before a purchase and before a paid reading, and refuses if the store does not confirm an adult.

New York's law on AI companions requires an age check beyond your own word before a reading. A connection does not show reliably which state someone is in, so Lumina asks for the check everywhere in the United States. k-ID runs it on its own page with the method you choose: a face estimate, your email address, a card or an identity document. Lumina learns only whether you are 18 or older. Until an adult is confirmed, Lumina neither reads nor sells to a connection or store account in the United States.

When someone may be in danger

Lumina follows one rule before every other instruction: a reading stops when the person is considering physically harming themselves or someone else, even without a plan, or when anyone seems to be in immediate danger. Ordinary anger or a figure of speech does not stop a reading; Lumina reads the person’s own words in context.

When the rule applies:

1. The reading ends at once. Lumina does not continue the reading or discuss the harm.
2. The cards are turned face down, and a message over them says to seek help now: the emergency number and the help line of your country, as set on your device, or findahelpline.com for other countries.
3. The conversation lists every help line in full. Each number can be touched to call it.
4. If the service cannot be reached at that moment, the app still shows how to get help.

At the end of a long conversation, a separate check looks at the latest message before the reading closes.

Health, law and money

A reading does not replace a doctor, therapist, lawyer or financial adviser. When a question turns on health, a legal matter or money, Lumina says so once and still helps you reflect on what is yours to decide.

Reporting a reply

You can report any reply from the reading screen. Reports are kept for 30 days and then deleted. Write to inbox@echoxstudios.art about anything else that concerns safety.
