About us and our approach to tarot

We offer personal reflection and modelling of life situations through tarot, for personal development and as a hobby or entertainment. This is not fortune telling. Lumina never foresees or promises anything. It claims no supernatural powers and does not offer to remove curses. The cards can help you explore a possibility or see a situation differently. A reader can be mistaken about a card, a person or a situation. Treat each interpretation critically. When a spread has a position called “future”, it opens a conversation about what could follow from the situation and choices you describe. A reading cannot verify someone’s motives, establish facts or promise an outcome. Check important facts independently before acting.

Neither an AI reading nor a human tarot session provides therapy, diagnosis, treatment, legal advice or financial advice. For a decision that calls for professional expertise, consult a suitably qualified professional. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Safety in Lumina describes how a reading stops when someone may be in danger and which help lines the app shows.

We test Lumina using fictional conversations. We ask for exact predictions and ask her to reveal someone else’s thoughts, including after she has already explained what a reading can offer. We also check that she can still give a useful interpretation when someone wants to explore a choice. These checks sample possible responses. An individual reply can still be mistaken. A reader may suggest a reasonable assumption and explain how acting on it could improve a situation. The people involved still decide how to respond.

Who gives your reading

A Lumina reading is a conversation with Lumina, the app’s AI tarot reader. You can draw from the built-in deck or photograph your own spread. Lumina can misunderstand an image or what you say. You decide which interpretations fit.

A live reading is an appointment with ECHOx or another human tarot reader. You meet inside the app by camera and microphone. The shared tarot board is the main workspace. A small video window in the corner lets you see one another while the reader moves cards, draws relationships and adds labels.

In either kind of reading you may share your question or keep it private. A private question gives the reader less direction at first and can leave more room for discovery through the cards and your conversation.

Who may enter a reading

You must be at least 18 to enter a reading. You decide which interpretations to accept, what to share and whether to act. You may question an interpretation or end the conversation. Confirming these points does not waive your statutory rights or release the provider from its legal duties.
