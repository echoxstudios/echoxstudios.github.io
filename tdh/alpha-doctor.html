The Alpha Doctor

✦

Not a real doctor. Real biscuits.

For anyone who carries power, desire, or a hypersexual way of living: hypermasculine, hyperfeminine, or however you carry it. Do you want to claim, own the room, command attention, be worshipped? Nothing you want counts against you here. The question is never whether to live it, only how: so that it keeps recharging you and the people you live it with, and is not lonely, not destructive, not isolating, but reinforcing.

Take one room at a time: a lover, a dynamic, friends, work, family, being alone. The same person can be a different Alpha in a different room.

A reading, not a diagnosis. No score, no verdict, no clinical advice. If someone is being hurt, stopping comes before anything on this page. If you are breaking, talk to someone you trust or a professional.
